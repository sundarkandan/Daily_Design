About Me

Patience is My
Greatest Lens

I'm Alex Wilder — a National Geographic-featured photographer who has spent fifteen years embedded in the world's most remote ecosystems. From frozen tundras of Svalbard to the dense undergrowth of Borneo, my work documents creatures few humans ever witness.

Great wildlife photography is 90% waiting and 10% pressing the shutter. I don't photograph animals — I photograph moments.
