About Pinnacle

A Legacy of Academic Excellence Since 1985

Pinnacle College of Excellence was established with a singular vision — to provide transformative education that equips students with knowledge, character, and the confidence to lead. Over four decades, we have evolved into a vibrant institution that blends tradition with innovation.

Our 120-acre campus is home to state-of-the-art laboratories, a world-class library, Olympic-standard sports facilities, and a thriving student community from over 30 states and 12 countries.
